📌 FACEBOOK Application:

> > Suppose I open the Facebook application. It's asking me to log in.

> > Here, Facebook is verifying who I am.

> > If I enter valid credentials, I will be allowed to log in.

> > This process is called Authentication.

✅ Authentication Means ===> Verifying Who You Are?

> > I'll show another example.
_______________________________________________________________________________

📌 I am Opening BOOKMYSHOW Application:

> > Suppose I access the BOOKMYSHOW application.

> > As a Normal User, I can browse movies,  I can select seats,  I can book tickets.

> > But can I add movies? No.

> > Can I update ticket prices? No.

> > Can I add theaters? Absolutely No.

> > Because I'm just a Normal User, but Admins can do that.

> > User 1: ADMIN

> > Add Movies, Update Ticket Prices, Add Theaters, View Revenue

> > User 2: NORMAL USER

> > Browse Movies, Select Seats, Book Tickets

> > Here, we are deciding what a user is allowed to do after login.

> > This process is called Authorization.

✅ Authorization Means ===> What Are You Allowed To Do?
